Play SENRITSU

旋律

01

Your music, your charts

Play 14 included songs, or import your own music. Keep the embedded title and artist, edit them, or enter your own song details before importing. Charts are built on your phone from the music itself, with hold notes and thumb-friendly patterns.

02

Find your challenge

Choose from Easy, Normal, Difficult, and Challenge. Set your note speed anywhere from 1.0× to 8.0×, and play at up to 120 FPS on supported screens.

03

Every beat in sync

Songs prepare silently before the countdown. A 20-second tap test helps line up the notes with what you hear through Bluetooth earbuds.

04

Make it feel like you

18 themes, including anime-inspired Filipino, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean scenes. Mix any background with 29 note styles: arrows, cultural icons, and the original SENRITSU mascot. Light and dark modes are free too.

05

Feel that perfect hit

Choose from 6 hit effects, from sparkles, petals and lightning to water ripples, manga bursts and 8-bit pixels. Celebrate PERFECT timing, combo milestones and full combos, or enable Reduce motion for gentler feedback.

06

Make every play count

Track your scores, play statistics, and achievements on your phone. Share a result card through the app you choose. No ads or tracking; gameplay works offline.
