SENRITSU – Rhythm Game

旋律 · A rhythm game for Android by Jake Side Quests

SENRITSU turns music into a rhythm game. Tap and hold the arrows as they reach the targets, play the six songs that come with the game, or import your own music and get charts in four difficulties: Easy, Normal, Difficult and Challenge.

Highlights

  • Charts built from the music itself, with hold notes and thumb-friendly patterns
  • A 20-second tap test that syncs the game to Bluetooth earbuds
  • Note speed from 1.0× to 8.0×, and up to 120 FPS on fast screens
  • Ten themes, light and dark modes, all free
  • Stats, achievements and shareable result cards
  • No ads, no tracking, works offline

Your first two song imports are free. SENRITSU Pro unlocks unlimited imports with one purchase, no subscription.
