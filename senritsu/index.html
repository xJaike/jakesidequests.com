Play SENRITSU

01

Your music, your charts

Play 5 included songs, or import your own music, several at once if you like. Keep the embedded title and artist, edit them, or enter your own song details before importing. Charts are built on your phone from the beat and the singing: every sound gets its note right on the beat, the same sound comes back on the same arrow, and both thumbs share the work.

02

Find your challenge

Play Classic on four arrows, or 5 lanes with a center lane between them that either thumb can play. Every song is rated by its own music: Easy, Normal, Difficult, Challenge, and Extreme. Set your note speed anywhere from 1.0× to 8.0×.

03

Every beat in sync

Songs prepare silently before the countdown. A 20-second tap test helps line up the notes with what you hear through Bluetooth earbuds. Play at up to 120 FPS on supported screens, with your thumbs, a Bluetooth controller or a keyboard.

04

Make it feel like you

18 themes, including anime-inspired Filipino, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean scenes. Mix any background with 29 note styles: arrows, cultural icons, and the original SENRITSU mascot. Light and dark modes are free too.

05

Feel that perfect hit

Choose from 6 hit effects, from sparkles, petals and lightning to water ripples, manga bursts and 8-bit pixels. Celebrate PERFECT timing, combo milestones and full combos, or enable Reduce motion for gentler feedback.

06

Make every play count

Star favorites and build playlists to play song after song. Track your best scores in each mode, play statistics, and achievements on your phone, and share a result card through the app you choose. No ads or tracking; gameplay works offline.
