SENRITSU Privacy Policy

Updated 20 October 2026 · App: SENRITSU (com.jakesidequests.senritsu) · Developer: Jake Side Quests

SENRITSU is a rhythm game. It is built to work entirely on your phone. This policy explains what that means for your information.

Information we collect

SENRITSU does not sell or share personal data. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game itself sends none of your information over the internet; the only exception is Google Play Games, which is optional and described below.

Songs you import

When you import an audio file, SENRITSU copies it into the app's private storage on your phone and analyses it on the device to build rhythm charts. Your songs are never uploaded or shared. You can delete an imported song inside the game at any time.

Scores and settings

High scores, play statistics, themes, note speed and audio calibration are saved only on your phone. They are removed when you uninstall SENRITSU. If Android's device backup is turned on, Android may include your game data in your own Google backup; the developer never has access to it.

Purchases

If you buy SENRITSU Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. SENRITSU only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro was purchased, so it can unlock Pro on your device. We never see your payment details.

Google Play Games (optional)

SENRITSU offers achievements and leaderboards through Google Play Games. When you are signed in to Play Games, Google receives your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), the achievements you unlock and, for the leaderboards, the total of your best scores on each difficulty of the songs included with the game. Google may show your leaderboard scores and profile to other players according to your Play Games visibility settings. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data for SENRITSU from your Play Games profile. Your play statistics, imported songs and settings are never sent; they stay on your phone.

Ratings

After a good result, SENRITSU may show Google Play's rating card. If you leave a rating or review there, it is handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; SENRITSU never learns whether you rated it.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share on the results screen, SENRITSU draws a picture of your score on your phone and opens Android's share menu. The picture goes only to the app you pick (for example Facebook or Messenger), and what happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy. SENRITSU itself uploads nothing.

Permissions

SENRITSU keeps the screen awake during play. When you import a song, Android's own file picker lets you choose one file; SENRITSU cannot see any other files.

Children

SENRITSU is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not collect information from anyone; the only data that leaves the phone is what Google receives through the optional Google Play Games and Google Play purchases described above.

Links

The "Send feedback" button opens the Jake Side Quests Facebook page in your browser. Anything you post or send there is covered by Facebook's (Meta's) privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message Jake Side Quests on Facebook, or use the developer email on SENRITSU's Google Play listing.
