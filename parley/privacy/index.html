Parley Privacy policy

How Parley handles your voice, camera, conversations, and information. The details, in plain words.

Parley is a translator built to work on your phone. Translation, text recognition and language detection run on the device. This policy explains what stays on your phone and the few times something leaves it.

Information we collect

The developer collects none of your information. Parley has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no server of its own. It does not sell or share personal data. Parley uses Google's ML Kit and your phone's speech services, described below. Those are the only times information can leave your phone.

Translation and language packs

Parley translates with Google's ML Kit, on your phone. The text you translate is not sent anywhere. Each language needs a language pack, which Parley downloads from Google when you add the language or first use it. In Offline Models you can limit downloads to Wi-Fi and delete packs you no longer need.

ML Kit may contact Google's servers for model updates and fixes, and it sends Google information about how its features perform, such as your phone model, Android version, the app's name and version, a per-installation identifier and processing times. It does not send the text, speech or images Parley processes. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, Parley uses your phone's speech recognition service, which on most phones is provided by Google. Your voice goes to that service while you speak. Depending on your phone, language and settings, it may recognize speech on the device or send audio to its provider's servers, under the provider's privacy policy. Parley receives only the recognized text. It does not record or keep audio. If that service is unavailable, Parley opens your phone's own voice input screen, which works the same way.

Spoken translations

Parley reads translations aloud with your phone's text-to-speech engine. Parley lists only voices that are installed on your phone.

Camera

Camera translation reads text from the camera preview on your phone. Camera images are never saved or sent anywhere; only the text you focus on is translated, on the device. Parley stops using the camera when you leave the camera screen or switch apps.

What stays on your phone

Your conversation history (what was said or typed, its translation and when), saved conversation names, favorite and custom phrases, tags, and settings such as your languages, voices and speech speed are saved only on your phone. You can delete a single line, clear your whole history, or delete custom phrases at any time. Uninstalling Parley removes everything.

Emergency medical card

If you fill in the emergency medical card, your blood type, allergies, dietary needs, medical notes and emergency contact are saved only on your phone. Parley shows, translates and reads the card aloud only when you open it, and it is never sent anywhere. You can edit the card at any time.

Backups

If Android's device backup is turned on, Android may include Parley's data, including your conversation history and medical card, in your own Google backup. The developer never has access to it. You can turn off backup in your phone's settings.

Sharing and copying

When you share a translation, export a conversation, or back up your phrasebook, Parley opens Android's share menu. The text goes only to the app you pick, and what happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy. Copy places text on your phone's clipboard.

Permissions

Microphone: for speech recognition. Camera: for camera translation. Both are optional; you can type instead. Internet and network status: to download language packs and for ML Kit as described above.

Children

Parley is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
