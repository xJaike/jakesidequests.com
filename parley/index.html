One phone.
Two languages.

Say it. Hear it back.

Speak in your language, and Parley shows and speaks the translation in theirs. When they answer, it does the same for you. Translation runs on your phone, in more than 50 languages.

01

Face to face

Set the phone between you. The screen splits in two, and the other half turns to face your partner, so you each read in your own language. Prefer one thread? Switch to Chat Stream.

02

Knows who’s talking

Turn on auto-detect and Parley works out which of your two languages is being spoken, so nobody has to switch first. Swap languages with one tap.

03

Hear it, too

Auto-Speak reads each translation aloud. Choose a voice, set the speed from 0.5× to 2.0×, and adjust the pitch. Show a translation full screen and flip it toward someone.

04

Point and translate

Aim the camera at a sign, menu, or page. Parley reads the text inside the focus frame, works out its language, and shows the translation below. It reads Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Devanagari scripts.

05

A phrasebook for the trip

Ready-made phrases for essentials, dining, transit, hotels, and medical help, with pronunciation guides. Star favorites, add your own with tags, and back them up as JSON or CSV.

06

Ready offline

Download the languages you need before you go, about 30 MB each, over Wi-Fi only if you like. Translation and camera reading then work without a connection.

If something goes wrong

Fill in the emergency medical card with your blood type, allergies, dietary needs, and an emergency contact. Parley translates it into the local language and can read it aloud slowly for a doctor or pharmacist.

Conversations, kept

Name your conversations, star the lines that matter, and search your history later. Export a transcript as formatted text, Markdown, or a clean summary.

What you need

Android 7.0 or newer. Microphone and camera are optional; you can always type. Each language downloads once, and speech recognition uses your phone’s own speech service.

Your words stay yours.
