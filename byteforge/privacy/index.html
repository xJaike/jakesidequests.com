ByteForge Privacy policy

How ByteForge handles your code, projects, and information. The details, in plain words.

ByteForge is a Java IDE that runs entirely on your phone. It does not ask for internet access, so it has no way to send your information anywhere. This policy explains what that means.

Information we collect

None. ByteForge has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share personal data. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so neither ByteForge nor the programs you run in it can connect to the internet.

Your projects

Your projects, their files, unsaved editor changes (kept so you can recover them), open tabs, and each project's run settings (program arguments, working folder and main class) are saved only in ByteForge's private storage on your phone. They are removed when you delete a project or uninstall ByteForge. ByteForge turns off Android's device backup, so your projects are not copied to your Google backup either. To keep a copy, export the project as a ZIP.

Importing and exporting

When you import a Java file or a project ZIP, Android's own file picker lets you choose one file, and ByteForge can read only that file. When you export, ByteForge writes only to the place you choose. It cannot see any other files on your phone.

Running your programs

ByteForge compiles and runs your code on your phone with its built-in Java runtime. Nothing is sent to a remote compiler. On phones with Linux kernel 5.9 or newer (most phones from the Android 12 era on), the compiler and your programs run in Android's isolated sandbox: they see only a copy of the open project and cannot read your other projects or ByteForge's files.

Older kernels, common on phones that launched with Android 10 or 11, cannot use this sandbox. There, your programs run with ByteForge's own access, so a program could read or change your other ByteForge projects, and the app shows a warning. They still cannot reach the internet or other apps' data. Only run code you trust.

Settings

Your app theme, editor theme, font size, Swing look and code-checking preference are saved only on your phone.

Copying output

When you tap Copy in the console, ByteForge places the program's output on your phone's clipboard, where you can paste it into another app.

Permissions

ByteForge requests no Android permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or shared storage.

Children

ByteForge does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Links

The Jake Side Quests link in the app opens jakesidequests.com in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message Jake Side Quests on Facebook, or use the developer email on ByteForge's Google Play listing.
